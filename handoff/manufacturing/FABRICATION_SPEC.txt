DESIGN RULES
------------
Minimum signal trace: 0.15 mm
Typical signal trace: 0.20 mm
Power trace:          0.50 mm
Typical via:          0.60 mm pad / 0.30 mm drill
Small J1 escape via:  0.40 mm pad / 0.20 mm drill
Power via:            0.80 mm pad / 0.40 mm drill

SPECIAL REVIEW ITEMS
--------------------
1. J1 is intentionally edge-mounted. Its contact pads and immediate escape
   traces extend into the board-edge clearance rule area.
2. J1 VBUS uses two 0.40/0.20 mm via-in-pad escapes. Confirm assembler process
   acceptance and solder-wicking risk.
3. Confirm selected SW1, SW2, and J2 parts match the footprints before order.
4. K1, D1, and J2 are through-hole parts and may require manual assembly.
